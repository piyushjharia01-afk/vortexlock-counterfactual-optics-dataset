# VORTEXLOCK Blind Optical Dual-Witness Corpus

Version 1.3.0

VORTEXLOCK is an original deterministic synthetic dataset for blind optical intervention algebra and dual-certificate synthesis. Each scene contains six coded intensity observations across eight propagation planes and eight candidate phase-plate controls. The prediction target selects an oriented three-control loop, forecasts its signed charged-braid circulation, and emits a unit functional certifying detector content outside a declared coordinate/nuisance explanation space.

The release asset is the participant-safe public corpus. It contains 1,024 labelled training scenes, 320 unlabelled test scenes, tensor observations, training targets and exhaustive training landscapes, a sample submission, and synchronized documentation.

## Source and generation

No external dataset, photographs, personal data, or model-generated content was collected. The corpus was generated deterministically in NumPy from a purpose-built optical simulator. It constructs six charged singularities, nine-plane trajectories, eight local controls, all 56 ordered two-control outcomes, analytic coded intensity frames, additive Gaussian measurement noise, uint16 quantization, and independently shuffled probe and candidate orders. The prepared target composes directed pair effects around each of 112 oriented three-control cycles. Train and test use disjoint compositional cells while retaining support for every marginal mechanism axis.

Complete generation semantics, schemas, units, tensor layouts, split construction, limitations, and intended use are documented in `DATASET_DESCRIPTION.md` and `DATASET_CARD.md`.

## Privacy boundary

The organizer archive, private answers, truth files, grading implementation, construction seeds, and creator kit are intentionally not public. They are not required to use the released participant dataset and publishing them would compromise the benchmark.
